BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY

A few useful answers.

What does one credit buy?

A credit is a usage unit, not a second of video and not cash. Aligning one song is 20 credits, including vocal separation and word-level timing. Unlocking browser video exports for that song is 10 credits. Together that is 30 credits, about $1 per song on the Creator plan ($9.99 for 300 credits). Further browser exports of an unlocked song are 0 credits. Cloud server rendering, when you choose it, uses compute credits.

How do credit reservations and expiry work?

Starting an alignment or a paid export reserves the quoted credits before the work runs. Success settles that reservation and does not charge it again. Failure or interruption releases the hold. Editing and previewing do not reserve credits. Monthly subscription credits expire at the end of the billing period and do not roll over. Expiring credits are used before permanent credits. The free credits on a new account are permanent and do not expire. A failed job cannot turn credits that have already expired into permanent credits.

Can I buy credits without a subscription?

Credit purchases are currently available only with a monthly subscription. The starting 60 credits do not require a card.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Sign in, open Quota & Credits, and choose Cancel renewal to use the secure billing portal. Cancellation and refunds are different: see our refund policy for payment assistance.

What happens if an export fails?

The application releases the reservation for a failed job. Monthly credits that have already expired cannot be converted into permanent credits. If a completed file is missing or unusable, contact support.

Do I need to own the music?

You need permission to process and use the music, lyrics, and artwork. Uploading a file or pasting a public Suno link does not itself prove copyright ownership. Fovea Studio does not grant music licences and is not affiliated with Suno. You are responsible for what you upload and publish.

How do I pay?

Sign in to the studio and select a package in Quota & Credits. When purchases are enabled, checkout is hosted by the secure payment provider identified before you pay. The final total, applicable taxes and supported payment methods are shown there.

Do the previews match exported videos?

The public pages use the same video component and layout code as Motion Studio and its exports. They show original sample lyrics with synthetic timings, without audio. Your own song, timing, palette and design settings determine your result.
