BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY

Questions, answered plainly.

Need a hand? Contact support or email support@fovea.si.

Do I need to own the music?

You need permission to process and use the music, lyrics, and artwork. Uploading a file or pasting a public Suno link does not itself prove copyright ownership. Fovea Studio does not grant music licences and is not affiliated with Suno. You are responsible for what you upload and publish.

Which audio formats are supported?

Upload MP3, WAV, or FLAC, up to 50 MB per file. You can also paste a public song link, including a Suno link, to import audio and lyrics. Lyrics can be pasted as text or uploaded as TXT or LRC.

Which lyric languages are supported?

Set the language to auto-detect, or choose English, Chinese, Japanese, or Korean before alignment. Auto-detect follows the lyric text. Those four are the explicit languages the aligner accepts. After alignment you can still correct any word.

How accurate is the alignment?

Alignment is word-level, after vocal separation, and it can be wrong. Long instrumental breaks, repeated sections, ad-libs, and lyrics that do not match the vocal are the usual trouble spots. The product does not guarantee a perfect sync, and the samples on this site use synthetic timings rather than a measured accuracy score. Listen through the song and drag a word if it is off, then export.

How do credits work?

Credits pay for processing. They are not cash. A new account receives 60 permanent credits once, with no card. That is 20 credits to align a song, including vocal separation, and 10 credits to unlock browser video exports for that song, so 60 credits cover about 2 songs. On Creator ($9.99 for 300 credits, 10 songs) a song is about $1 per song. After a song is unlocked, further browser exports of that song in 16:9 or 9:16, other layouts, and clips are 0 credits. Cloud server rendering, when you choose it, uses compute credits. The credit price of a song stays 30 credits on every plan.

How do credit reservations and expiry work?

Starting an alignment or a paid export reserves the quoted credits before the work runs. Success settles that reservation and does not charge it again. Failure or interruption releases the hold. Editing and previewing do not reserve credits. Monthly subscription credits expire at the end of the billing period and do not roll over. Expiring credits are used before permanent credits. The free credits on a new account are permanent and do not expire. A failed job cannot turn credits that have already expired into permanent credits.
